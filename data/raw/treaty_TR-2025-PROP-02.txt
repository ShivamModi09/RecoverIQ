Reinsurer: Swiss Re Corporate Solutions | Intermediary Broker: Guy Carpenter
Effective Period: 2025-01-01 00:01 Local Time to 2025-12-31 24:00 Local Time

=== [PAGE 3] ===
ARTICLE IV — RETENTION AND LIMIT
The Company (Insurer) shall retain as its initial net loss retention the sum of USD $1,000,000.00 per event/risk.
The Reinsurer shall be liable in respect of each loss occurrence for the excess of the Company's net retention up to a maximum limit of liability of USD $5,000,000.00.

=== [PAGE 5] ===
ARTICLE VIII — COVERED PERILS AND EXCLUSIONS
Covered Perils: Fire, Lightning, Explosion
Explicit Exclusions: Flood, Earthquake, Terrorism

=== [PAGE 6] ===
ARTICLE XI — LOSS OCCURRENCE DEFINITION (HOURS CLAUSE)
All individual property claims arising out of one atmospheric or catastrophe event within a consecutive 24-hour period shall be deemed one single Loss Occurrence for retention and limit aggregation.
