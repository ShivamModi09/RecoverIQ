=== [PAGE 5] ===
ARTICLE IV — RETENTION AND LIMIT
The Company (Insurer) shall retain as its initial net loss retention the sum of USD $15,000,000.00 per event/risk.
The Reinsurer shall be liable in respect of each loss occurrence for the excess of the Company's net retention up to a maximum limit of liability of USD $20,000,000.00.

=== [PAGE 8] ===
ARTICLE VIII — COVERED PERILS AND EXCLUSIONS
Covered Perils: Windstorm, Fire, Winter Storm, Explosion, Wildfire
Explicit Exclusions: Flood, Earthquake, Cyber

=== [PAGE 11] ===
ARTICLE XI — LOSS OCCURRENCE DEFINITION (HOURS CLAUSE)
All individual property claims arising out of one atmospheric or catastrophe event within a consecutive 168-hour period shall be deemed one single Loss Occurrence for retention and limit aggregation.
